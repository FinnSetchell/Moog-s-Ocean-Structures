### Added

- `mos:sunken_ship` (weighted pool of 2 multi-piece wood variants: spruce and birch, each with `/rear` + `/front`)
- `mos:rare_travel_ship` (multi-piece: `/front` + `/rear`)
- `mos:docked_spruce_ship` (multi-piece: `/main` + `/dock`, moored with pillared supports)
- `mos:hauling_ship_1`
- `mos:ship_small`

- `mos:ship_wreck_spruce`
- `mos:ship_wreck_dark_oak`

- `mos:ruined_house` (weighted pool of 20 sea-floor ruin variants: `ruined_house_1` through `ruined_house_20`)

Mobs:

- Long ships are crewed by a pirate raiding party: each ship spawns a named `Ship Captain` (a vindicator flying a skull-and-crossbones banner and carrying an enchanted axe) plus 4 raiders drawn from a weighted pool of pillagers, vindicators and evokers.
- `mos:mobs/wandering_trader` — rides the wanderer rafts, with randomised trades per spawn
- `mos:mobs/drowned` — spawns around the ruined houses and Atlantis ruins
- `mos:mobs/axolotl` — random colour per spawn

Loot:

- Themed loot tables throughout, covering ship holds and cargo, captain's quarters, sunken cabins, ruined houses, Atlantis ruins, the church, the castle, the nether portal archway, wanderer raft cargo, buried treasure and assorted decor.
- Suspicious gravel can be brushed for finds in the dragon skeletons and ruined walls.
